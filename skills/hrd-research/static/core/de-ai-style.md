# De-AI Style Pass

Use this file for revision, polish, restructuring, and any request to make prose sound more natural.

The goal is not to make prose casual. The goal is to keep scholarly HRD writing while removing generic AI fingerprints.

## What To Remove Or Reduce

Avoid overused AI-like vocabulary and filler such as:

- "delve into"
- "underscore"
- "pivotal"
- "realm"
- "landscape"
- "robust"
- "nuanced" when vague
- "multifaceted"
- "transformative"
- "leverage" when "use" is enough
- "foster" when the actor/action is unclear
- "crucial" or "vital" without evidence
- "plays a significant role"
- "sheds light on"
- "navigate the complexities"
- "not only ... but also" when it creates artificial balance

Do not ban these words mechanically. Remove them when they make prose sound generic, inflated, or detached from the author's specific claim.

## Rhythm And Flow

Watch for:

- repeated sentence openings
- overbalanced clauses
- stacked abstractions
- three-part lists that sound manufactured
- transitions that summarize instead of advancing logic
- paragraphs that feel too smooth but say too little
- generic ending sentences that merely restate importance

Prefer:

- clear subjects and verbs
- varied sentence length
- concrete HRD constructs and contexts
- transitions that show reasoning
- modest, evidence-aware claims
- the author's natural emphasis when it is clear

## Preserve

Preserve the user's:

- intended argument
- citations and citation placement
- construct names
- disciplinary terminology
- study details and findings
- cautious uncertainty
- distinctive phrasing when it is understandable and appropriate

## Output Behavior

When revising, do not announce a long de-AI diagnosis unless the user asks. Just make the prose sound less generic.

If the source text has strong author voice, make lighter edits.

If the source text sounds highly AI-generated, revise more decisively and mention briefly that the pass reduced formulaic phrasing and rhythm.
